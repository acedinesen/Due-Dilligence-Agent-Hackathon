## 1. Deina Kellezi — Investor, byFounders

### Background
Deina Kellezi is Albanian by origin, raised in Copenhagen, and joined byFounders as an Investor in December 2025 [web:60]. Her path into venture started outside tech entirely — she briefly considered medicine before joining a software company in a commercial role after high school, where she "fell in love with the builder mentality" [web:60]. That pulled her into computer science with a focus on AI and algorithms; she taught courses and founded a mentorship nonprofit for young engineers along the way [web:60].

Her professional career includes Microsoft and GitHub, working on developer experience, followed by a stint at **BCG**, where she advised on GenAI strategy for pharma clients and contributed to Denmark's national AI strategy [web:60][web:61]. byFounders Partner Sara Rywe described the hiring process as a multi-year search for "a technical yet strategic person in Copenhagen" who was also a cultural fit, calling Deina "a needle in a haystack" [web:60]. Outside of work, she is a musician and standup comedian, plays Starcraft, Half-Life, CS and League, and describes herself as "a nerd" who solved a Rubik's cube in under 30 seconds [web:60].

### Investment Focus and Judging Criteria
Kellezi is explicit about what excites her: companies "using AI in novel and efficient ways," particularly through proprietary data, improved inference, or deep embedding into customer workflows — often surfacing in complex industries like healthcare or drug discovery, though she isn't sector-locked [web:60]. She also has a specific soft spot for teams challenging the status quo of social networks and online communities, an area she notes isn't a common investor focus [web:60].

Beyond the product, she is drawn to founders who have faced real adversity — "moving constantly, personal struggles or even a failed company" — and can articulate how that history shaped their conviction to build something bold [web:60]. Authenticity matters to her personally and professionally: she says she refuses to have a "work persona" and values honesty and directness in how people show up [web:60].

Her first public deal at byFounders was leading/supporting the **€3.8M seed round into Tangled**, a Finland-based AI-native code collaboration platform. Her public remarks on that deal praised the founders' "deep passion, speed of shipping, and vision" [web:58][web:59][web:66]. byFounders itself invests pre-seed through Series A across the Nordics and Baltics, sector-agnostic but software-focused, with average ticket sizes of EUR 500K–4M; the fund carries an Article 8 (ESG-promoting) classification and counts Lovable, Intric, Willo Technologies, DataCrunch, and Arsenale Bioyards among its portfolio [web:64][web:78].

### What to Expect
- She responds to technical depth — as an engineer herself, she'll engage substantively with how something is built, not just the market narrative [web:60].
- She's candid about being "terrible at email" and juggling a lot — patience and light follow-up, rather than pressure, is likely to be well received [web:60].
- Her stated ambition for the next few years is "meeting more founders" and pointing to companies where she made "a real and trajectory-changing impact" — she is actively building her own track record and is motivated to find those stories [web:60].

## 2. Milo (Milo AI 🧢) — AI Founders Network

### Background
Milo is a Denmark-based (Aarhus) serial founder and operator with over 15 years of entrepreneurial experience [web:105]. His own account describes 10+ businesses built, 100,000+ customers served across ventures, over $10 million raised, and five exits [web:103][web:105]. He frames his entrepreneurial streak as lifelong — running a hair salon at boarding school, selling coffee at a hospital, and flipping secondhand road bikes as a teenager, eventually scaling to selling a hat to Pharrell Williams and building multimillion-dollar companies [web:103][web:105].

For eight years he worked as a **fractional co-founder and advisor**, helping early-stage founders go from zero to one, raise capital, and scale toward an exit [web:103][web:105]. Most recently, he served as **Head of Growth at Riff.ai** through 2025, where he grew revenue by 500%, helped close a $16M Series A, scaled the team from 5 to 20+, led a pivot and rebrand, and closed multiple six-figure enterprise deals [web:103]. He studied at IBC International Business College (HHX) [web:105].

### Current Activity
Milo now runs **vibe coding meetups internationally** and works with VCs and AI startups at seed-to-Series-A stage, describing his approach to growth as "unconventional" and built on networking, storytelling, and "IRL experiments & stunts" [web:103][web:105]. His self-description centers on documenting everything and converting stories into inbound interest [web:103].

Through his platform "AI Founders Network," he has been organizing a dense cluster of events tied to TechBBQ 2026 (Aug 26–27, Bella Center Copenhagen), including a "VC Hackathon: Build AI Agents For VCs," a "VC x AI Founder Mixer" attended by Antler, byFounders, Morgan Stanley, HSBC Innovation Banking, and Deutsche Bank, and a "TechBBQ Midnight Buildathon" — often co-hosted with AI Öresund, GAIIN, Cursor, and Supabase [web:88][web:91][web:94][web:95].

### Judging Criteria
Milo's public content signals that he values momentum, visible building, and storytelling over formal pitch polish — his events are structured around getting founders to build and ship live (hackathons, buildathons) with investors and banks in the room to observe [web:91][web:95]. Given his growth-marketing background and fractional co-founder history, he is likely to evaluate people on execution speed and the strength of their narrative/positioning as much as the underlying product.

## 3. Arlon den Teuling — Co-Founder, AI Öresund; CEO, PhaseOne AI

### Background
Arlon den Teuling was born and raised in the Netherlands, where he built communities and worked in entrepreneurship education, before moving to Sweden in late 2017 [web:101][web:102]. He has described the Öresund startup community as feeling like a "first home base" upon arrival, and went on to work in a startup before moving into consultancy [web:101].

In November 2022 he became **CEO of Skåne Startups**, a grassroots entrepreneurial organization founded in 2015 [web:101]. Under his leadership the organization's direction shifted from grassroots community-building toward a support system for already-established companies — a shift he later said didn't match where he wanted the ecosystem to go [web:104][web:106]. Skåne Startups was discontinued in late 2023, described publicly as "the end of an era," with a call for "bold founders to revitalize" the ecosystem going forward [web:106].

Following that, in January 2024, den Teuling co-founded **Malcon Invest** with Marco Muraca — the name blending "Malmö," "Marco," and "Arlon" [web:102][web:104]. Muraca brought 25 years of investment banking experience from Lehman Brothers, Morgan Stanley, and Saxo Bank, having worked in London, Geneva, and Copenhagen [web:102]. Malcon Invest's mission was to raise capital for scale-up companies while also offering legal, patent, and financial services support — den Teuling explicitly cited a lack of investor access for ambitious regional founders as the gap he wanted to close [web:102][web:104].

His current ventures are **PhaseOne AI**, which he leads as CEO since January 2025 — an AI consulting and automation agency based in Malmö helping accountancy firms and "ambitious everyday businesses" adopt practical AI, with the tagline "Making AI Boring & Profitable" [web:63][web:80][web:83] — and **AI Öresund** (formerly AI Builders, rebranded June 2026), which he co-founded to become the largest AI network spanning Copenhagen and Skåne [web:63][web:68]. His PhaseOne co-founders include Art Mashanin (ex-Microsoft/Pipedrive) and Abhay Chatterjee [web:80]; AI Öresund's team includes Wala Loubani (ex-Google, founder of Karzone.ai) and Erik Johansson [web:68].

### Judging Criteria
Den Teuling's founding thesis for PhaseOne AI was a direct reaction against industry hype: "Too many were hyping unproven tech and treating automation like a buzzword," which pushed him to focus specifically on "the boring parts of AI" that deliver provable results for traditional businesses [web:80]. This suggests he evaluates ideas on measurable operational ROI and real workflow fit rather than narrative or vision alone — consistent with his recent work running structured "AI Opportunity Assessment" interviews and hands-on workshops with client teams [web:84].

His history with Skåne Startups and Malcon Invest also shows a recurring pattern: he gravitates toward closing structural gaps in the regional ecosystem — first grassroots community, then capital access for scale-ups, now practical AI adoption and cross-border network-building [web:101][web:102][web:104]. He is likely to respond well to pitches framed around solving a genuine, underserved gap in the regional market rather than abstract ambition.

## 4. Matthias (Mattias) Wickenberg — Inter IKEA Group; Board Member, MurrAI Skania AB

### Background
Mattias Wickenberg currently holds the title **Roadmap Leader**, Roadmap and Portfolio Management, at **Inter IKEA Group**, a position he has held since October 2025, based in the Greater Malmö area [web:49]. His career path within Inter IKEA includes Capability Planning Leader in Range Operations (January 2024–October 2025) and Business Architect (January–December 2023) [web:49]. Earlier in his career, he held Project Leader and Scrum Master roles, and was connected to **IKEA Food**, where in 2017 he presented a digitalization collaboration between IKEA and the software company Barium [web:50][web:52].

Separately from his day job, he has served as a **board member at MurrAI Skania AB** since January 2025 [web:49].

### Judging Criteria
Nothing in his public record points to an investment mandate, business development remit, or capital-allocation authority — his entire visible career is operational and technical: roadmap management, capability planning, business architecture, and agile delivery inside a large-scale corporate retail organization [web:49][web:50]. If he weighs proposals at all in his Inter IKEA capacity, it would likely be through a feasibility, process-rigor, and scalability lens rather than a financial-return lens.

His board seat at MurrAI Skania AB is the only concrete thread connecting him to any kind of capital or governance decision outside his employee role — no further public detail on that company's business activities or his specific responsibilities there was found, which would need direct confirmation (e.g., via Swedish company registry Bolagsverket, or asking him directly) before treating him as an investment contact.
